# Narration Script — "The Man Who Carried a City"
## Canon Scene 003

**Voice:** Tai's Professional Voice Clone (ElevenLabs Turbo v2)
**Tone:** Low, warm, deliberate. Not performance — recognition. Speaking to someone who is carrying something they haven't named yet.

---

## Full Film Narration (35 seconds / 7 shots)

He carried the city before he knew its name.

Not the streets or the spires — the weight behind them. The agreements no one wrote down. The dependencies that held even when the bridges didn't.

Every morning, the same stone. The same strike. The same answer from above — light, recognition, the city saying *I see you* without saying *put me down*.

People watched him carry. Some saw a man with a burden. Some saw a man with a map he hadn't learned to read yet.

The question was never whether the city was too heavy.

The question was whether he knew he was allowed to build a road that didn't pass through his own chest.
